About Exaryn

Exaryn is a one-person research & development studio run by Chinmay Purohit in Melbourne. It started as a home for side projects — AI agents one month, derivatives pricing the next, a webcam party game somewhere in between — and grew into a place to build, learn, and share the results.

The approach is simple: build things, ship them, and be honest about how they went. Some projects are polished and live on the internet; others are early prototypes still finding their shape. Every one of them taught something, and they're all in the index.

The studio is happily AI-native — on the order of a billion Claude tokens this year, turned into working software. (That figure is an estimate, and the front page says so; nothing reports it back.) The daily signal exists because keeping up with the AI world is a job in itself, so a robot compiles the best of it every morning.

THE THINGS THAT RECHARGE THE WORK.

  • Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

    Chess you play with your whole body. Leverage over strength — and no better training for staying calm inside a hard problem.

  • AFL

    Chaos with structure. A whole city holding its breath over one bounce of an unpredictable ball.

  • Hiking

    The best debugger I own doesn't run on a laptop. Problems untangle themselves around the second kilometre.

  • Gym

    The most honest feedback loop of the week. The weight goes up or it doesn't — either way it tells the truth.

  • Reading

    A decade of someone's thinking for the price of a paperback. Paper only — the battery never dies.

  • Latte

    Small, daily craftsmanship. The warm pause that starts the thinking.
